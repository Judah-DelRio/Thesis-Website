The 16 Styles

A style is how a player plays, not how good he is; most players are a blend of two or three of them. The numbers on each card are how much of each of six traits the style shows, in standard deviations from the average NBA player: four traits on offense and two on defense. Zero is average; a higher number means more of that trait, not a better player. They describe how a player plays, not how well. Pick a card to open it.
