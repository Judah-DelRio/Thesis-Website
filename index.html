The 16 styles

A style is how a player plays, not how good he is; most players mix two or three. The numbers on each card are how much more or less the style leans on each tendency than the average NBA player, in standard deviations: Movement Shooter’s +1.6, −0.7 and −0.9 mean far more off-ball shooting, less on-ball creation and less paint presence than average. They are differences in tendency, not in skill. The set is grouped by the tendency each style leans on most. Pick a card to open it.
